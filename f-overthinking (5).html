👌 Ah-ha! Welcome to this super short course built for you. Designed to help you halt overwhelm, stifle stress and pull you out of the malaise of middle age (if you're there).

When you believe your own thinking, you are at its mercy. It's easier to fall into the trap of thinking 'things out there can impact how I am feeling in here.'

The principles in this course have allowed me to navigate three cancer diagnoses — well, two when you factor in I learned a lot of this because of the first diagnosis. BUT — if they worked for me when I was due to have my jaw ripped out and rebuilt with a bone from my leg, I am confident they'll work for you too.

It doesn't matter if you're a CEO, in sales, working from home, junior staff, director level or setting up your own business — if you're a human, this will help you.

The goal of this mini-course is to give you the tools, tips and understanding to spot when your mind is being unhelpful (for want of a stronger word) and for you to live your life, on your terms.

And not on the terms of the tyrant within.

Ready? Let's begin…

Shaun
